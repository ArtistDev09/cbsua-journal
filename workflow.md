# Editorial Workflow Overview

This document outlines the main interactions between the Author, Reviewer, and Admin in the journal submission process.

## 1. Author Workflow

1. The Author submits a manuscript through the journal system.
2. The submission is received and entered into the editorial workflow.
3. The Author can track the status of the submission from the dashboard.
4. The Author may receive review requests, revision requests, and editorial decisions.
5. If revisions are required, the Author uploads revised files and responds to reviewer or editor feedback.
6. Once the submission is accepted, the Author may be involved in final publication preparation.

## 2. Reviewer Workflow

1. The Admin or Editor assigns a Reviewer to the submission.
2. The Reviewer receives a review request and can accept or decline it.
3. If accepted, the Reviewer reads the manuscript and submits a review report.
4. The Reviewer may recommend acceptance, revision, or rejection.
5. The review result is sent back to the editorial team for decision-making.

## 3. Admin Workflow

1. The Admin manages journal settings, users, and editorial roles.
2. The Admin or Editor creates and assigns submissions to the appropriate reviewers.
3. The Admin monitors the overall workflow and ensures tasks move forward.
4. The Admin reviews reviewer reports and editorial decisions.
5. The Admin can approve final publication steps and manage the published issue.

## 4. Interaction Summary

- Author → submits the manuscript and responds to feedback.
- Reviewer → evaluates the manuscript and provides recommendations.
- Admin → oversees the process, assigns reviewers, and finalizes editorial decisions.
- The workflow is cyclical: submission → review → revision → decision → publication.

## 5. Typical Lifecycle

1. Submission received
2. Reviewer assigned
3. Review completed
4. Revision requested or accepted
5. Final decision made
6. Publication prepared
